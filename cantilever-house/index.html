The house brief

The Cantilever House is a two-story modern house. A glass-and-concrete ground floor wraps a courtyard with a lap pool, and a cedar-clad bedroom wing sits on top, reaching 20 ft past the end of the house.

All dimensions are in feet. The origin is the southwest corner; +X is east and +Y is north.

PartWhere (x, y in ft)Height
Living wing (ground)0–60 × 0–2411 ft; the living room (x 0–20) is double-height to 21 ft
Guest and garage wing (ground)0–24 × 24–5811 ft, flat roof

That is about 3,200 sq ft of living space plus a two-car garage. Exterior walls are 10 in thick and interior walls are 5 in.

How to use the prompts

Paste the six prompts into Claude Code one at a time, in order, with FreeCAD or BIMCraft open and connected. You need:

  • FreeCAD with the FreeCAD MCP add-on, or BIMCraft, which has its AI connection built in
  • Claude Code on a paid Claude plan

The rules I followed in the video:

  • Paste each prompt exactly as written.
  • If Claude asks a question, answer “Use your best judgment.”
  • If the program crashes, reopen your saved file and tell Claude to continue from where it left off.
  • Save after every prompt.

The prompts below are worded for FreeCAD. For BIMCraft, change only the first sentence of Prompt 1 to:

In the project open in BIMCraft, model a modern house called the Cantilever House.

The six prompts

1

The ground floor

In the active FreeCAD document, model a modern house called the Cantilever House using the BIM workbench. Use real BIM objects (Site, Building, Levels, Walls), not plain solids, and work in feet. The origin is the southwest corner, +X is east, +Y is north.

Create a Site and a Building with two levels: Ground at 0 ft and Upper at 11 ft.

On Ground, build 10-inch exterior walls for an L-shaped plan:
- Living wing: 60 ft x 24 ft, from (0,0) to (60,24). The west 20 ft is a double-height living room, so its walls rise to 21 ft. The other living wing walls are 11 ft.
- Guest and garage wing: 24 ft x 34 ft, from (0,24) to (24,58). Walls are 11 ft.
2

The cantilever

Now the signature move. On the Upper level, build a 60 ft x 24 ft bedroom wing from (20,0) to (80,24). It sits on top of the living wing and cantilevers 20 ft past its east end, with nothing underneath. Give it a floor slab at 11 ft and 10-inch exterior walls 10 ft tall. Also add a ground floor slab under both ground-floor wings.
3

The plan

Lay out the interiors with 5-inch walls, and label every space.
- Ground, living wing, west to east: double-height living room (x 0-20), dining (x 20-36), kitchen (x 36-52), pantry and powder room (x 52-60).
- Ground, guest wing: guest suite with bath (y 24-36), two-car garage (y 36-58).
- Upper: a 6 ft corridor along the north side. South of it: bedroom 2 (x 20-32), bedroom 3 (x 32-44), shared bath (x 44-52), primary bath (x 52-60), and the primary bedroom filling the cantilever (x 60-80).
- A straight stair, 4 ft wide, against the north wall of the dining area from x 22 to x 36, rising from Ground to Upper.
4

Glass, doors, roofs, pool

Make it feel like architecture, not a box:
- Living room: floor-to-roof glass on its west and south walls.
- Courtyard: floor-to-ceiling glass on the living wing's north wall from x 24 to x 60, and on the guest wing's east wall.
- Primary bedroom: the whole east end wall of the cantilever is glass.
- Upper south wall: one continuous ribbon window, 3 ft tall.
- A front door in the south wall at about x 28, a 16 ft garage door in the garage's west wall, and interior doors to every room.
- Flat roofs: one roof at 21 ft over the living room and the bedroom wing with a 3 ft overhang, and a flat roof at 11 ft over the guest and garage wing.
- An 8 ft x 30 ft lap pool in the courtyard, from (48,26) to (56,56).
5

Make it beautiful

Give it materials: board-formed gray concrete for the ground-floor walls, warm cedar for the bedroom wing's exterior, dark bronze window frames, clear blue-tinted glass, and blue water in the pool. Then set up a dramatic hero view from the southeast, a little below eye level so the cantilever reads, and save it as a high-resolution image named hero.png.
6

Drawings and export

Create dimensioned floor plan drawings for the Ground and Upper levels, plus one section cut through the living room and the cantilever. Then export the whole model as an IFC file named cantilever-house.ifc.

Two changes worth making

These prompts are exactly what I ran, including the mistake from the video. Make these two changes before you use them on anything real.

Fix the garage door. In this brief the garage touches only the guest suite, and Prompt 4 asks for interior doors to every room. Both programs put a door from the garage straight into the guest bedroom. Add this sentence to Prompt 3:

Add a mudroom between the garage and the house.

Make the AI tell you what it assumed. Add this sentence to the end of every prompt:

List any choices you made that I didn't specify, and any problems you see.

Then read the AI’s notes and review the model yourself: exits, code and structure. The AI does the modeling, and the design review stays with you.
